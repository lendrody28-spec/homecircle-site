Other support

Find grief support, community groups and domestic violence help through official finders and services.

HomeCircle doesn't run, host or list these groups. We link to official finders and services so you can find support near you. HomeCircle is independent and isn't affiliated with or endorsed by any service we link to.

If you're under 18, Kids Helpline is there any time on 1800 55 1800.

Grief counselling and grief support groups

Griefline is now part of SANE Australia. Call 1300 845 745, Monday to Friday, 10am-8pm (AEST/AEDT). Griefline's online bereavement groups are for people aged 18+ living in Australia. They are not a local-area finder. Griefline says these groups are free.

Call Griefline 1300 845 745Griefline

A Friend's Place, operated by the National Centre for Childhood Grief, provides counselling and group support for children, teens and their families. Parents can find out more on their site. Their site says specialist counselling for children and teens is free. Call 1300 654 556.

Call 1300 654 556National Centre for Childhood Grief (A Friend's Place)

Grief Australia (the Australian Centre for Grief and Bereavement) has bereavement counselling and support groups. On that page the number is (03) 9265 2100. Specialist bereavement support is funded by the Victorian Government and is free of charge to Victorians. It is not a national postcode finder.

You can also search for local support groups with healthdirect's support groups finder or the Australian health services search.

Men's groups

The Australian Men's Shed Association has a find a shed tool to look up a shed near you. The Department of Health, Disability and Ageing page on men's sheds lists the Association on 1300 550 009.

Call Australian Men's Shed Association 1300 550 009Find a shed

MensLine Australia is a telephone and online counselling service for men, not a local men's group finder. MensLine says it offers free professional 24/7 telephone counselling. Call 1300 78 99 78.

Call MensLine Australia 1300 78 99 78MensLine Australia

Relationships Australia has support services in each state and territory. To be connected to the nearest Relationships Australia, call 1300 364 277. Their services are for all members of the community.

healthdirect's support groups finder, linked above, can also be used to look for local groups.

Women's groups

There isn't one national directory of women's groups. These finders can help.

  • healthdirect support groups finder
  • healthdirect women's health clinic finder
  • Relationships Australia, including 1300 364 277

If you're worried about your safety, see domestic violence support and 1800RESPECT on 1800 737 732.

LGBTQ+ groups

QLife provides anonymous and free LGBTIQ+ peer support and referral. QLife phone and webchat are open from 3pm – 9pm every day. Call 1800 184 527. QLife is not a postcode map. QDirectory lists national and state organisations.

Call QLife 1800 184 527QLife get helpQDirectory

LGBTIQ+ Health Australia has an LGBTI Supports Navigator to find services.

The Rainbow Sexual, Domestic and Family Violence Helpline, from Full Stop Australia, is for anyone in Australia from LGBTQ+ communities who has experienced sexual, domestic or family violence, and for supporters. Call 1800 497 212.

Call 1800 497 212

Multicultural and migrant support

TIS National (Translating and Interpreting Service) is provided by the Department of Home Affairs. For immediate phone interpreting, call 131 450. TIS National says this is available 24 hours a day, every day of the year. See also the Home Affairs page on TIS National and TIS National contact details.

Call TIS National 131 450

The Department of Home Affairs lists Settlement Engagement and Transition Support (SETS) providers. There is also a SETS program overview. HomeCircle doesn't list provider addresses.

Domestic violence

If you're in danger now, call 000.

1800RESPECT is the national domestic, family and sexual violence counselling, information and support service. It is available for free, 24 hours a day, 7 days a week. Call 1800 737 732.

The Rainbow Sexual, Domestic and Family Violence Helpline is on 1800 497 212.

For types of abuse, effects on children, official help and safe-browsing notes, see domestic violence support.

Call 000Call 1800RESPECT 1800 737 732Domestic violence support
