# HomeCircle website (homecircle.au)

Free website for the HomeCircle recovery support app (au.endrody.homecircle), served by GitHub Pages at https://homecircle.au. Static HTML, CSS and JavaScript only: no accounts, no cookies, no analytics, no trackers, no third-party scripts or fonts.

## Pages
- index.html – Home, with Upcoming events (from events.json)
- library.html and library/*.html – the app's Library (guides, breathing, meditation, body, fellowships, recovery books)
- twelve-steps.html – the 12 Steps in HomeCircle's own plain-language words (not the official AA/NA wording), with a link to aa.org
- meditations.html – animated breathing guide and meditations
- meetings.html – meeting finder that reads the app's public feed in the browser
- help.html – every helpline in the app's HelpDirectory, Family & Partners, GA state contacts, and alcohol and drug lines by state
- your-circle.html – optional day count, journal and health safety notes, stored only in the browser's localStorage
- support.html – contact and FAQs

Every page has the coral "Help" pill in the top corner (Call 000, Call Lifeline 13 11 14, and a link to the Help page), as in the app, and Kai, the recovery buddy: a pure in-browser port of the app's KaiEngine and KaiFeelings (assets/kai-engine.js, data in assets/kai-data.js generated by build.py). Kai makes no network calls and keeps its chat in localStorage. Test it with `node tools/test_kai.js`.

Meeting reminders download an .ics file with an alert 2 hours before the meeting (single or weekly). No push notifications and no server.

Browser storage keys: hc.kai.v1, hc.kai.pos.v1, hc.health.v1, hc.circle.v1, hc.journal.v1. Nothing is sent anywhere.

## Updating
- Content comes from the iOS app source: `python3 tools/extract_app_content.py /path/to/homecircle-ios > tools/app-content.json`
- Events: edit `events.json` (title, start, end, place, state, fellowship, organiser, source). Only add events from official AA or NA pages. Past events hide themselves.
- Rebuild pages: `python3 tools/build.py`, then check links: `python3 tools/check_links.py`

The Privacy Policy currently links to https://lendrody28-spec.github.io/homecircle/privacy.html and the Terms of Use to https://lendrody28-spec.github.io/homecircle/terms.html. The site has no privacy page of its own.

The app's meeting feed is served separately from lendrody28-spec/homecircle (gh-pages) at https://lendrody28-spec.github.io/homecircle/feed/au/index.json. Do not move it. This site only reads it.
