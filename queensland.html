Finding addiction support in Queensland

HomeCircle is a free, independent app and website that helps people find support near them. HomeCircle isn't affiliated with or endorsed by Alcoholics Anonymous, Narcotics Anonymous, Gamblers Anonymous or Porn Addicts Anonymous. HomeCircle is support, not medical care.

Find a meeting

Alcohol and drug support in Queensland

Adis 24/7 Alcohol and Drug Support is a free, confidential line for Queenslanders with alcohol or other drug concerns, and for the people who care about them. It's open 24 hours a day, 7 days a week. Call 1800 177 833.

Call 1800 177 833Adis website

The National Alcohol and Other Drug Hotline on 1800 250 015 connects you to your state's alcohol and drug service.

Call 1800 250 015

How HomeCircle can help

The HomeCircle iPhone app is free on the App Store (there is no Android app). The Library has guides, breathing and meditation. The meetings map on this website and in the app can help you find meetings; most meetings link to their official listing. You can also chat with Kai, an automated recovery buddy. Kai isn't a person or a counsellor, and isn't for emergencies.

Frequently asked questions

Is HomeCircle free?

Yes. The website and the iPhone app are free. There are no accounts, no sign-in, no ads and no trackers. Anything you save on the website stays in this browser, and anything you save in the app stays on your phone.

Does HomeCircle run meetings?

No. We link to each group's own meeting finder.

Who is HomeCircle for?

HomeCircle is for people looking for recovery support, and for families and friends. The app is for adults 18+. Our For families & partners pages are open to everyone.

For any health concern, talk to your GP or a doctor. HomeCircle is support, not medical care.

HomeCircle is independent and isn't affiliated with or endorsed by Alcoholics Anonymous, Narcotics Anonymous, Gamblers Anonymous or Porn Addicts Anonymous.

If you're unsafe, call 000 or Lifeline 13 11 14.
