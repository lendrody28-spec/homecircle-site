Get help

1800RESPECT is the national domestic, family and sexual violence counselling, information and support service. It is available for free, 24 hours a day, 7 days a week. Call 1800 737 732, or text 0458 737 732. 1800RESPECT also lists NRS: 1800 555 677 and Interpreter: 13 14 50.

1800RESPECT can help you plan for your safety.

Call 1800RESPECT 1800 737 7321800RESPECT Service Directory1800RESPECT website

Use the Service Directory on 1800RESPECT's site to find services. HomeCircle doesn't list addresses or safe-house locations, and doesn't map locations.

The Rainbow Sexual, Domestic and Family Violence Helpline is for anyone in Australia from LGBTQ+ communities who has experienced sexual, domestic or family violence, and for supporters. Call 1800 497 212.

Services Australia has information, referrals and support for people affected by family and domestic violence, including help with payments.

Types of abuse

1800RESPECT describes domestic and family violence as a repeated pattern of behaviour that happens when one person in a relationship hurts another or makes them feel unsafe. Abuse doesn't have to involve physical violence. Read more on 1800RESPECT's domestic and family violence page.

1800RESPECT describes psychological abuse as when someone uses words or behaviours to make you question your feelings, memories, or perception of reality to control or isolate you.

1800RESPECT describes physical abuse as when someone deliberately hurts or threatens to hurt any part of your body, or takes away your control over your body.

1800RESPECT describes financial abuse, also known as economic abuse, as a form of domestic and family violence that often involves someone using money in ways that can hurt and control you.

1800RESPECT describes coercive control as an underpinning dynamic of family and domestic violence, where a person uses patterns of abusive behaviours over time that create fear and deny liberty and autonomy. The Attorney-General's Department has fact sheets on understanding coercive control.

Effects on children

Children can be affected by violence at home, even when it isn't aimed at them, including by seeing or hearing it. Support is available for children and for parents. 1800RESPECT explains more. Young people can call Kids Helpline any time on 1800 55 1800.
