Breathing

Breathing · 3 min

Box breathing

A steady 4-4-4-4 count. In, hold, out, hold. Stop whenever you want.

Breathing · 3 min

4-7-8 breathing

In for 4, hold for 7, out for 8. A longer out-breath.

Breathing · 3 min

Slow out-breath

In for 4, out for 6. No hold. The gentlest of the three.
