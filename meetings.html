Meeting reminders

Choose Add reminder on a meeting, then Just this one or Every week. This downloads a calendar file (.ics) for your calendar app. Its alert is set for 2 hours before the meeting starts. HomeCircle sends no notifications, and nothing is sent to us.

Don't share your full name, address, phone, bank details or passwords with anyone in HomeCircle or at meetings. Real friends and sponsors won't ask for money or private details. If someone does, tell your sponsor or a trusted person.
