Answers below are for HomeCircle 1.2.16 for iPhone, the current App Store version.

Do I need an account or to sign in?

No. HomeCircle has no accounts and no sign-in. There's no email, password or verification code. Open the app, do the short setup, and everything stays on your phone.

How do I find meetings?

Open Meetings. The app downloads a public meeting list (no personal details are sent). Tap Near me if you'd like meetings sorted by distance in km. Your location is only used when you tap it, and it stays on your phone. Listings come from public sources (BMLT, Meeting Guide and public AA/NA listings), so times can change. Check with the group if you're unsure. Spotted a wrong listing? Email us.

Is my journal private?

Yes. Your journal is stored only on your phone and is never uploaded.

Can other people see my posts or messages?

Not in this version. Your profile, posts and messages stay on your phone and aren't sent to anyone else's phone or to us.

Who can see my sober date, faith or culture?

No one, unless you choose to show it. Sober date and badges are hidden by default, and Faith/beliefs and Culture are set to "Don't share" by default. You can change these any time in your profile.

How do I report, block or mute someone?

Tap ⋯ on a post, message or profile and choose Report, Block or Mute. These work on your phone to hide content. Reports are saved on your phone only. Nothing is sent to us and nobody at HomeCircle reviews them. If someone may be in danger, call 000, or Lifeline on 13 11 14.

How do I export or back up my data?

In the app, open Settings, go to Your data and tap Export my data. HomeCircle makes a file on your phone. Use Share to save it somewhere you trust, such as your Files app or a drive. The file isn't password-protected, so keep it safe. Your iPhone's normal iCloud backup may also include HomeCircle data if you have backup turned on.

How do I move my data to a new phone?

Export your data on the old phone (see above) and copy the file to the new phone. Then, on the new phone, open Settings → Your data → Import from file and choose the file. Import replaces the HomeCircle data on that phone, so you'll be asked to confirm first.

How do I delete my data?

Open Settings → Your data and tap Delete account, then confirm. There's no online account. This erases the HomeCircle data stored on your phone. We don't hold a copy, so there's nothing left on our side. Deleting the app also removes its data from your phone. If you want to keep anything, export it first.

Is Kai a real person or AI?

Neither. Kai, the Recovery Coach, puts together replies on your phone from built-in templates. It makes no network calls, and nothing you type to it leaves your phone. Kai isn't a counsellor, doctor or sponsor. If you're struggling, reach out to your sponsor or your GP, or call Lifeline on 13 11 14.

Does HomeCircle show ads or sell my data?

HomeCircle has no ads, doesn't track you across other apps and never sells your data.
