On screen:
Have this read to you. Your phone can read any page out loud, including this one. iPhone or iPad: go to Settings > Accessibility > Read & Speak (on older versions, Spoken Content) and turn on Speak Screen. Then swipe down with two fingers from the top of the screen, or say “Siri, speak screen”. Android: go to Settings > Accessibility > Select to Speak and turn on the Select to Speak shortcut. Tap the shortcut, then tap what you want read. If you can’t find it, install Android Accessibility Suite from Google Play.
