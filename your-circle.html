Your circle

A day counter, a private journal and health safety notes, if you'd like them. All optional.

Stored only in this browser. Anything you save on this page stays in this browser's local storage, on this device. It isn't sent to HomeCircle or anyone else, and it isn't shared with the app or your other devices. Private browsing or clearing your browser data removes it. On a shared or public computer, anyone using this browser could read it, including your journal and health answers. Use private browsing, or tap Clear everything on this browser when you're done.

This browser isn't letting the site save anything (it may be in private mode or have storage turned off). You can still use the page, but nothing will be kept.

Day count

Set a sober date when you want a day count.

Day 1 is your sober date itself, as in the app. The day count is kept in this browser only.

Health safety

Always talk to your doctor. This is not medical advice. Answers stay in this browser.

Health notes

Skip breath holds if you have heart, lung or blood pressure conditions, or are pregnant. If you tick heart or blood pressure, a lung condition, pregnancy or panic attacks, HomeCircle hides breath holds and offers the slow out-breath instead, as the app does.

A private journal, just for you

Don't share your full name, address, phone, bank details or passwords with anyone in HomeCircle or at meetings. Real friends and sponsors won't ask for money or private details. If someone does, tell your sponsor or a trusted person.
