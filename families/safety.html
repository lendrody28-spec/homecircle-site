Worried about violence at home?

If someone you love is drinking or using, and you feel unsafe, you deserve help. You can call any of these services.

1800RESPECT

Anyone affected by domestic, family or sexual violence, and people supporting them. National.

Hours: 24/7

Call 1800 737 732Open site

Last checked: 4 Nov 2026

Kids Helpline

Children and young people aged 5–25.

Hours: 24/7

Call 1800 55 1800Open site

Last checked: 4 Nov 2026

Men's Referral Service (No to Violence)

For men worried about their own behaviour, and their family, friends and professionals.

Hours: 24 hours 7 days a week

Call 1300 766 491Open site

Legal questions

Questions about protection orders, custody or making someone get treatment need proper legal advice. We can't give legal steps. Please don't monitor or track someone's phone or messages.

Queensland: Legal Aid Queensland's Legal Information Line gives free legal information (Mon–Fri 8:30am–4:30pm AEST, except public holidays).

Legal Aid Qld 1300 65 11 88Legal Aid Qld site

Other states and territories: National Legal Aid links to all 8 state and territory Legal Aid commissions, with a Find legal help page.

HomeCircle

Support for your recovery journey. Free, with no accounts, no sign-in, no cookies, no ads, no trackers and no analytics.

For any health concern, talk to your GP or a doctor. HomeCircle is support, not medical care. HomeCircle is not a medical device and doesn't give medical advice. In an emergency call 000. Lifeline 13 11 14.

HomeCircle is independent and isn't affiliated with or endorsed by Alcoholics Anonymous, Narcotics Anonymous, Gamblers Anonymous or Porn Addicts Anonymous.
