Supporting someone through withdrawal

Withdrawal warning signs and when to call 000

Withdrawal from alcohol or some medicines can be dangerous. Don't manage it at home alone. See a GP, or call the National Alcohol and Other Drug Hotline on 1800 250 015 (some state lines have set hours). Call 000 for a seizure, confusion or collapse.

Call 000 straight away if they:

  • have a seizure (fit)
  • are confused or don't know where they are
  • collapse or can't be woken

More info

When someone cuts down or stops alcohol or some drugs, their body can react badly. Don't try to manage it at home on your own.

The hotline is open 24/7 and puts you through to your state or territory's service. SA calls are answered 8.30am–10pm.

If opioids are involved, naloxone can reverse an overdose for a short time. It's free, with no prescription, at participating pharmacies and other approved sites.
